# Planet Fitness 4-Day Mass Plan

## General Guidelines
- **Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes before lifting[cite: 1].
- **Rest Periods:**
  - 2 minutes after compound lifts (presses, rows, leg press)[cite: 1].
  - 60–90 seconds after isolation exercises[cite: 1].
- **Intensity:** Train with 1–2 reps in reserve (RIR)[cite: 1].
- **Progression:** Increase weight when you hit the top of the rep range with good form[cite: 1].
